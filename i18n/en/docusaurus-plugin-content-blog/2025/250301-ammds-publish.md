## An exciting moment

We are delighted to announce that AMMDS (Adult Movie Meta Data Scraper) v1.0.0 has officially been released and entered public beta!

{/* truncate */}

> **In one sentence**: AMMDS is a film library you keep on your own device. It recognizes the messily named videos on your drive, works out which film each one is, then adds the cover, description, cast and tags — turning them into a library you can browse, search and maintain over time. All of it stays on your own machine.

### What this release can do

v1.0.0 is the first version released to the public, with the core workflows up and running:

- **Metadata scraping**: identify films by ID and automatically fill in covers, descriptions, cast, tags and more from metadata sites — no more hunting for images and copying synopses one by one.
- **Film and actor records**: gather everything you organize into a unified library you can browse, search and maintain.
- **Site resource search**: connect to private trackers (PT sites) and search for resources directly in AMMDS (site authentication was formally opened up in the later v1.0.24).
- **Web management interface**: manage your library from a browser — accessible from PC, tablet or phone, and well suited to running long-term on a NAS.

### About the public beta

Starting today, AMMDS v1.0.0 enters public beta. We invite everyone to try it and share feedback.

- **Beta period**: ongoing over the coming months;
- **Continuous updates**: we keep fixing issues and improving features based on feedback, with steady version bumps;
- **Stable release**: a stable version will follow after the beta.

> Requirement: AMMDS is an offline product, and data is entered and maintained by the user. Please comply with the laws of your region and do not promote this product within China.
