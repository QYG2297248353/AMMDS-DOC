## Officially Established

Xinjiang Mengsen Software Development Studio officially announces its establishment today, marking an important step forward for us in the field of software development.

If you're interested in how the studio came to be, you can start with [The Story Behind the Studio](../2023/230615-behind-the-studio.md).

### About Us

The studio focuses on providing high-quality software development services to our clients and is committed to advancing the industry through technological innovation. We have a passionate and professional team, dedicated to building solutions that meet our clients' needs.

### Business Scope

**General items:**
- Software development
- Network technology services
- Information technology consulting services
- Artificial intelligence application software development
- Software and services related to blockchain technology
- Internet sales (except for goods whose sale requires a license)

(Except for projects subject to approval by law, business activities shall be carried out independently in accordance with the law on the basis of the business license.)

**Licensed items:**
- Internet information services

(For projects subject to approval by law, business activities may be carried out only after approval by the relevant authorities, and the specific scope of business shall be subject to the approval documents or licenses issued by the relevant authorities.)

### Looking Ahead

We will uphold the philosophy of "innovation, professionalism and service", continuously improving our technical capabilities and service standards, creating greater value for our clients and contributing to the development of the industry. We look forward to moving ahead together with you and building a better future!
